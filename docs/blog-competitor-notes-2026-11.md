rewriting, noting strengths, weaknesses and the information-gain gap each rewrite fills. Logged here
per post as the rewrite happens (backfilled for posts 1-6, live going forward for post 7+).

## Post 1 — are-electric-dirt-bikes-street-legal (`street legal electric motorcycle for sale`)

Top results: ZILOX E, MotoEbikes, Bennetts, Flex Electric, Zapmap — all UK-focused (MSVA approval, UK
licence categories A/A1/A2, UK Plug-in Motorcycle Grant). **Gap filled**: none address the US federal

rewrite does, and none name specific currently-stocked street-legal models with direct purchase links
